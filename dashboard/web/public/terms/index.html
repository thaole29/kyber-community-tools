2. What the bot does

The bot works in the background. It has no commands and does not post in the server. It records support-ticket activity (when a ticket is opened, answered and closed), reads a small set of public community channels to produce an anonymised daily summary, and sends internal reports and alerts to the support team. It never sends you direct messages.
