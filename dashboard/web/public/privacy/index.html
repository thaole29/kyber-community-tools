KS Support Analytics bot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

1. Who runs this bot

The KS Support Analytics bot is run by the community team of the "Kyber Network Official" Discord server, led by the community moderator. This policy covers both Discord applications that make up the bot: Analyze Bot (application ID 1474841004959076472) and KS_Community_Backup (application ID 1559480430594367488). They do the same job and store data in the same place. It is a community tool, not a commercial product: it is installed only in this one server, is not offered publicly and cannot be added to other servers.

The bot runs on the moderator's personal computer and uses an internal AI gateway (details in section 4). Only the community team uses the data it collects.

The bot exists for one reason: to help us serve and support our community better. It shows us how quickly and how well members get help, so we can fix slow spots, cover busy hours and answer common questions before they become tickets.

Contact: open a support ticket in the server. This is the only contact channel for privacy questions and requests.

2. What we collect

  • Discord user IDs of ticket owners, ticket closers and support staff.
  • Ticket channel names and timestamps (created, first response, closed, deleted).
  • In support ticket channels only: the user's first message (up to 200 characters) and a short excerpt of the user's messages (up to 2,000 characters).
  • In a small list of public community channels: the last 24 hours of messages, read only to produce an anonymised daily summary. Raw community messages are not stored.
  • While a ticket is waiting for a staff reply: the member's latest message in that ticket, so we can tell a new question from a closing "thank you". It is cleared as soon as staff reply.
  • Ticket transcripts that Ticket Tool saves in our staff-only archive channel: read to judge whether the member's issue was resolved. We store a satisfaction label, a score and up to four short notes, which can quote the member.
  • A lookup table of short messages (up to 200 characters, with links, wallet addresses and mentions removed) and whether each one needs a reply, so the same short message is not sent to the AI twice. It holds no user IDs.
  • Counts of support staff messages per community channel.

We do not collect presence, direct messages, IP addresses or email addresses, and we do not request the Server Members intent. We never ask for wallet details; if a member writes a wallet address in a ticket, it is stored only as part of that message text.

3. Why we use it

To serve and support our community better. Concretely, we measure response time and quality (first response time, SLA breaches, who handled and closed a ticket), assign each ticket one product category, tell whether a member's latest message still needs a reply, judge whether a closed ticket was resolved to the member's satisfaction, and produce internal reports for the support team. Staff can also search these records with an internal question-and-answer tool. We do not use the data for advertising, profiling of members, or sale, and we do not use it to train machine-learning or AI models.

We process this data on the basis of our legitimate interest in providing good support to members of our Discord community.

4. Who we share it with

  • Amazon Web Services (Amazon Bedrock, model Claude Sonnet), reached through an internal AI gateway — the only AI service that receives message text. It receives ticket text to return a category label, whether a message needs a reply, a satisfaction label, and answers to staff questions; and community messages (with usernames replaced by anonymous IDs such as user_1) to return a summary. Bedrock does not use inputs or outputs to train models.
  • Google (Gemini embeddings) — builds the search index for the internal question-and-answer tool. It receives only ticket labels (ticket number, category, staff member, satisfaction label), public KyberSwap documentation and staff search questions — never member message text.
  • Telegram — internal reports and alerts for the support team. An alert for a ticket waiting over 15 minutes can include the user's first ticket message.
  • Hosting: the moderator's personal computer, with FileVault full-disk encryption.

These providers may process data outside your country. We only use providers bound by written data-processing terms. Public dashboards contain aggregated numbers, ticket numbers, staff names and anonymised community summaries only — never member message text, usernames or user IDs.

5. How long we keep it

  • Ticket records (timings, categories, Discord IDs and the short message text listed in section 2): kept while the bot is used for support reporting, so we can compare trends over time.
  • Community summaries: anonymised, with no usernames or user IDs. Raw community messages are not stored.
  • You can ask us to delete your data at any time — see section 7.

6. Security

Data is encrypted at rest. Access is limited to the community moderator who runs the bot. Bot tokens and keys are kept out of source control. If we learn of unauthorised access to your data, we will tell affected members and Discord promptly.

7. Your rights

You can ask for a copy or deletion of your data by opening a support ticket in the server. We reply within 30 days. Deletion removes your message text and replaces your Discord ID with a random token, so ticket statistics stay correct but can no longer be linked to you. Messages already delivered to Telegram cannot be recalled.

8. Children

The bot follows Discord's minimum age. We do not knowingly process data of users under that age.

9. Changes

Updates are posted on this page with a new date and announced in the server.
